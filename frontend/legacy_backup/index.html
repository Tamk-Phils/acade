AcadFormat Privacy Policy & Legal Regulations

Governed by Cameroonian Law No. 2010/012 on Cybersecurity and Cybercrime

1. Statutory Framework and Preamble

AcadFormat is the official academic document restructuring and identity compliance platform of the University of Bamenda (UBa), Cameroon. This Privacy Policy outlines the terms governing the collection, processing, and protection of user data and academic manuscripts under Law No. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on Cybersecurity and Cybercrime in Cameroon and Law No. 2010/013 on Electronic Communications.

2. Purpose of Document Processing

Uploaded documents (theses, dissertations, project reports, internship reports, and course assignments) are parsed solely for the technical execution of layout compliance: enforcing UBa 4.0 cm inside binding margins, Times New Roman 12pt typography, bilingual preliminary pages, and structured tables of contents. No manuscript content is sold, redistributed, or utilized to train external public language models.

3. Device Binding & Account Integrity

To maintain institutional security, prevent unauthorized account sharing, and ensure fair usage of the 72-hour free trial, accounts are bound to a persistent digital hardware identifier upon authentication. Accessing an account from an unregistered device triggers automatic verification and paywall re-authorization.

4. Mobile Money Transactions & Financial Confidentiality

Payments of 250 FCFA for 7-day export passes processed through MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) and Orange Money are conducted via encrypted telecom gateways. AcadFormat does not store banking PINs or confidential telecom security credentials.

5. Academic Intellectual Property

All intellectual property rights in manuscripts submitted to AcadFormat remain exclusively with the author and the University of Bamenda.

6. User Rights to Rectification and Erasure

In accordance with Article 34 of Law No. 2010/012, students and researchers hold the right to review, rectify, or request the purging of their uploaded files and account records from the AcadFormat data registry.
